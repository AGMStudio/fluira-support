Before you troubleshoot

Fluira has no account or server copy of your history. If the app has been deleted and you did not make a JSON backup, the developer cannot recover missing local records.

Do not send your activity history, notes or exported files through a public support channel.

How do I create or edit an activity?

In Today, tap + and choose Habit or Routine. Give it a title, days, start date, icon and color. A habit has a daily goal from one to twenty; a routine has two to thirty ordered steps. Tap an existing activity’s options to edit it.

If today already contains progress for an activity, a recurring edit may start tomorrow so the record you already made remains coherent.

Can one habit need several checks and reminders?

Yes. Set the daily goal to the number you need. You can optionally add up to one reminder time for each daily check, so a three-times-per-day habit can remind you morning, midday and evening. Reminders are optional and scheduled locally.

I checked something by mistake. Can I undo it?

Yes. Use the temporary Undo action after a check. You can also long-press the habit for a deliberate correction. For an older date, open History and use the correction action for that day.

How do I reorder habits or routines?

Drag the small handle at the top of a card within its section. Habits and routines remain separate, and completing something does not reorder the list. A long press opens correction actions instead.

How does a step-by-step routine work?

Open a routine from Today or its reminder. Tap individual steps to correct them in any order, or use Next to complete the first pending step. Your progress remains available when you leave and return.

A scheduled routine can opt into a Live Activity. On supported systems it shows the current step and a simple Next action on system surfaces. Advanced edits still happen inside Fluira.

Why did a reminder or Live Activity not appear?

In Fluira, confirm that notifications and Live Activities are enabled as needed and that the activity has a time. Then check the independent settings for Fluira in iOS Settings. Focus modes and system notification settings can silence or delay alerts. Fluira uses local scheduling and has no push server.

How do I correct a forgotten past day?

Open History, choose week, month, quarter or year, select the relevant date and use the correction action. You can update forgotten habit checks or routine progress without changing today’s recurring plan.

How do I add or refresh an iPhone widget?

Complete Fluira’s initial setup first. Then edit the iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen, choose to add a widget and search for Fluira. Home Screen and accessory families show bounded Today information, and supported widgets offer a validated quick habit action.

If a widget looks out of date, open Fluira once. Widget refresh timing is ultimately managed by iOS. Discreet mode reduces the details shown on system surfaces.

My Apple Watch does not show the current state. What can I do?

Confirm that the Fluira Watch app is installed from the Watch app on your iPhone and that the Watch is paired and reachable. Open Fluira on the iPhone, then on the Watch. The iPhone is the source of truth; supported Watch actions reconcile when the devices can communicate.

How do I back up or move my data?

In Settings → Your data, export a JSON backup and save it somewhere you control. On the destination iPhone, import the backup, review the preview, then merge or replace local data. The CSV export is a readable table for analysis; it is not a complete restorable backup.

How do I erase all Fluira data?

In Settings → Your data, choose Erase all data and confirm. This removes activities, history, preferences, local companion state and notifications controlled by Fluira, then returns to initial setup. Files you exported elsewhere are not deleted.

Can I change appearance, time format or the first weekday?

Yes. In Settings, choose automatic, light or dark appearance; 12- or 24-hour time; and Monday, Saturday or Sunday as the first day of the week. These choices apply throughout Fluira and its relevant companion views.

Where can I get help with a purchase, download or refund?

Apple processes App Store purchases and refunds. Use Apple’s Report a Problem service or Apple Support for transaction and download issues. Fluira has no in-app subscription or additional purchase.

Learn and privacy

The Learn tab offers concise, original habit guidance and links each topic to its scientific sources. It is general educational content, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

For details about local storage, exports and device companions, read the privacy policy.

Contact support

Open a Fluira support request on GitHub. Include the Fluira version, iOS version, device model and a short description of what happened.

GitHub issues are public. Never include activity titles, notes, history, exported files or other sensitive information.
