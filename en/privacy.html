At a glance

Fluira does not collect or transmit your habit and routine records to the developer. Activities, schedules, checks, routine steps, history and preferences are processed locally to provide the features you choose.

Last updated: 27 August 2026.

1. Scope and responsible party

This policy describes data processing performed by the Fluira iPhone app, its widgets, Live Activities and its companion app for Apple Watch. It does not cover processing performed independently by Apple or by a website you choose to open under its own terms.

Responsible party: Adrian Guerra Marrero
Privacy contact: use the public Fluira support channel on GitHub. Do not include private or sensitive information.

2. Information kept on your devices

Fluira may store the following information locally because you create or configure it:

  • habit and routine titles, notes, icons, colours, active days, start dates and reminder times;
  • daily goals, ordered routine steps, checks, sessions, corrections and day states;
  • appearance, language, time format, first day of the week, notifications, haptics, celebrations and discreet-display preferences;
  • the minimum technical identifiers, generations and command receipts needed to keep app, widget, Live Activity and Watch actions consistent and apply them only once.

Fluira does not ask for your name, email, age, sex, weight, location, contacts, photos or Health data. It does not create an advertising or clinical profile.

3. No developer collection or tracking

Fluira has no user account or developer backend. It contains no advertising, third-party analytics, third-party crash reporting, tracking, IDFA integration or CloudKit synchronization. The app’s privacy manifests declare no tracking and no data collected by the developer.

4. Widgets, Live Activities, Apple Watch and notifications

  • Widgets: receive only a bounded Today snapshot through Fluira’s local App Group. They do not read or export your complete history. Interactive habit actions are written back through validated local commands.
  • Live Activities: are optional for scheduled routines. They show the minimum current routine state needed for progress and a Next action. iOS may present that state on supported system surfaces, including the Apple Watch Smart Stack.
  • Apple Watch: receives the Today state needed for the companion experience and sends limited commands through Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. The iPhone remains the source of truth; the Watch does not receive your complete history.
  • Notifications: are scheduled locally by iOS. Permission is optional and requested only after you choose a reminder. The system may mirror notifications to a paired Apple Watch.

These components retain only the local snapshots, preferences and bounded command records needed for their functions. None of this information is sent to a Fluira server.

5. Exports, imports and external sources

You can create a complete JSON backup or export a readable CSV. A file leaves Fluira only when you choose a save location or share it through the system sheet. It is then under your control and the privacy terms of any destination you select; Fluira cannot delete copies stored elsewhere.

Learn contains optional links to specific scientific sources. Opening one sends the destination an ordinary web request through the system, which may include information such as your IP address under that site’s policy. Fluira does not add your activities, history or an advertising identifier to the link.

6. Your controls and retention

Local information remains until you edit or erase it. You can undo a recent check, correct previous days, export a backup, import a supported backup in merge or replace mode, or choose Erase all data. Full erasure removes activities, history, preferences, companion state and notifications controlled by Fluira, then returns the app to initial setup.

Because Fluira has no account or backend, the developer cannot view, recover or synchronize records from your device. Keep your own JSON backup before deleting the app or changing devices if you want to retain your history. Files previously exported elsewhere are not erased by the app.

7. Permissions and device security

The only user permission expected in version 1.0 is notifications. Core tracking and history continue to work if you decline it. Live Activities also respect the independent system setting. Fluira relies on Apple app containers and system frameworks to separate its local data from other apps. You are responsible for protecting access to your unlocked devices and exported files.

8. Apple services

Apple may independently process App Store purchases, downloads, device diagnostics, backups or system services under Apple’s privacy terms. That processing is not performed by Fluira’s local data store and does not give Fluira an account containing your activity history.

9. Children and sensitive uses

Fluira is a general organization and education tool. It is not designed to collect children’s personal information and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or medical monitoring. Do not store information in activity titles or notes that you would not want visible to anyone with access to your unlocked device or its system surfaces.

10. Policy changes

If Fluira’s data practices change, this page and its update date will be revised before a release that relies on the change. A future feature that collects or transmits data would require a new review and clear disclosure.

Privacy questions
